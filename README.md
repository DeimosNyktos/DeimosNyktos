### Carnet de Français

<img src="https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/760/noanimate/https://deimosnyktos.github.io/carnet-de-francais/" alt="Carnet de Français screenshot" width="100%" />

Learn French with **spaced repetition** and **CEFR levels (A1–C2)**.
Interface in Catalan, Spanish and English. Works **offline** and installs as a **PWA**.

- Single-file app, no frameworks, no build step
- Flashcards scheduled by spaced repetition
- MIT licensed
